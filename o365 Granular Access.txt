Microsoft Entra Conditional Access gives you fine‑grained control over which Office 365 apps users can access and under what conditions.

🎯 Short Answer
You can create Conditional Access policies that:

Allow Outlook and Teams on unmanaged devices

Block all other Microsoft 365 apps (SharePoint, OneDrive, Office web apps, etc.)

Optionally require approved client apps or App Protection Policies for Outlook/Teams on personal devices

This is fully supported and widely used in BYOD environments.

🔍 How It Works (Based on Microsoft documentation)
1. Conditional Access lets you target specific cloud apps
You can scope policies to individual Microsoft 365 services — not just the whole suite.
Microsoft’s documentation confirms you can block or limit access to specific apps such as Exchange Online or SharePoint Online independently. 

2. You can block access from unmanaged devices
Conditional Access supports blocking access to Microsoft 365 apps unless the device is:

Intune‑enrolled

Marked compliant

Hybrid‑joined

Using approved client apps

This is the standard method for restricting BYOD access. 

3. You can allow Outlook and Teams while blocking everything else
You simply create:

Policy A (Allow)

Target apps: Exchange Online and Microsoft Teams

Condition: Unmanaged devices

Grant: Allow access (optionally require App Protection Policies)

Policy B (Block)

Target apps: Office 365 or specific apps (SharePoint, OneDrive, Office Online, etc.)

Condition: Unmanaged devices

Grant: Block access

Microsoft guidance shows how to block specific apps such as SharePoint or OneDrive while allowing others. 

4. Browser access can be blocked separately
You can block browser access to Outlook and Teams while still allowing the native apps — meaning you can tighten controls even further if needed. 

5. App Protection Policies (MAM) add extra BYOD security
If you want Outlook and Teams to be allowed on personal devices but protected, Intune App Protection Policies can enforce:

PIN requirement

Prevent save‑as to personal storage

Restrict copy/paste

Block jailbroken/rooted devices

These apply without requiring device enrollment. 

🧭 Recommended Architecture for Your Requirement
✔ Allow on unmanaged devices:
Outlook (Exchange Online)

Teams

✘ Block on unmanaged devices:
SharePoint Online

OneDrive

Office web apps (Word/Excel/PowerPoint Online)

Any other Microsoft 365 cloud apps

✔ Optional enhancements:
Require approved client apps (Outlook/Teams only)

Require App Protection Policies for data protection

Block browser access entirely for unmanaged devices

🧪 Deployment Best Practices
Start in Report‑only mode to validate impact before enforcing. 

Always exclude break‑glass accounts. 

Test with a pilot group of unmanaged devices.

📌 Bottom Line
You absolutely can restrict unmanaged devices so they only access Outlook and Teams.
Microsoft 365 access control is granular, not all‑or‑nothing.
